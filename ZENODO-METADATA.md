**Description (HTML o testo):**

Exact verification code for the paper "Near-tight values of the Lonely Runner
spectrum for five and six speeds" (F. Cordella, 2026). It contains: the
classification of the two-dimensional subtori of (R/Z)^6 with D(U) = 1/3 and of
(R/Z)^5 with D(U) = 3/10 (Python, exact rational arithmetic); the exact
maximum-loneliness kernel in C; the complete Jain-Kravitz sector analysis of the
subtorus U_C with explicit thresholds (Python); the one-very-fast-runner checks;
and the exhaustive searches over all tuples of four to eight speeds up to the
bounds stated in the paper. `run_all.sh` reproduces every number quoted in the
paper in about twenty minutes on two cores. Requirements: Python 3 (standard
library; numpy optional) and a C99 compiler with OpenMP.
